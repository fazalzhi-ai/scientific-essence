Before You Submit Your Manuscript (Click here)

Please use the following instructions to prepare a clear, consistent and professionally presented manuscript. Scientific content and research structure may be appropriate to your discipline.

1. File requirements

  • Submit the manuscript in PDF format only.
  • Maximum file size: 10 MB.
  • Ensure the PDF is readable, complete and properly formatted.
  • Use clear text and avoid blurred images or scanned pages wherever possible.

2. Page formatting

  • Paper size: A4.
  • Font: Times New Roman or Arial.
  • Main text: 12 point.
  • Main headings: 14 point, bold.
  • Subheadings: 12 point, bold.
  • Line spacing: 1.5.
  • Text alignment: justified.
  • Maintain consistent margins and paragraph spacing.

3. Headings and subheadings

Use a clear and consistent hierarchy. Number headings and subheadings in sequence. Keep headings concise and meaningful. Use bold formatting to distinguish headings, and avoid excessive colors, decorative fonts or unnecessary highlighting.

1. INTRODUCTIONMain heading, bold and uppercase1.1 Background of the StudyFirst level subheading1.2 Objectives of the StudyFirst level subheading1.2.1 Specific ObjectivesSecond level subheading2. MATERIALS AND METHODS2.1 Study Design2.2 Data Collection

4. Tables, figures and images

  • Number tables and figures consecutively.
  • Provide a clear title or caption for each table and figure.
  • Refer to each table or figure in the manuscript text.
  • Ensure images and diagrams are clear and readable.
  • Identify reproduced material and obtain permission where required.

5. References

  • Include references for all cited sources.
  • Use a consistent referencing style throughout the manuscript.
  • Ensure every in text citation has a corresponding reference entry.

6. Author contact information

Provide a valid email address in your registered account. An optional WhatsApp number may be provided in the submission form to help the editorial office contact you about your manuscript. Contact details are confidential and used only for journal administration and editorial communication. They are not shared with peer reviewers or published.

7. Final PDF checklist

  • Check spelling, grammar, heading sequence and numbering.
  • Verify tables, figures and references.
  • Confirm that no pages or content are missing.
  • Confirm that the final PDF is no larger than 10 MB.

Your registered email address will be used for official editorial communication. Your email and WhatsApp number are visible only to authorized editors and administrators, not reviewers.
